## 1. Purpose — CONFIRMED (from `caveats`/`dataset_notes`, verbatim)

> "PROTOTYPE — NOT A PRODUCTION BANKING FRAUD MODEL. Trained and evaluated
> ONLY on S40 synthetic data generated by ml/data/generators, because no
> public dataset was available under the project's data-governance rules
> (IEEE-CIS licence PENDING; PaySim and others require an authenticated
> manual download that has not been performed). Every metric here
> therefore measures the model's ability to recover patterns a generator
> deliberately put there. It is NOT evidence of real-world
> fraud-detection accuracy and must never be presented as such. Real
> performance is unmeasured."

This model emits **`fraud_probability` only** — never a risk score, risk
level, or decision. Fusion (Phase 6) owns combining it with the anomaly,
device and voice signals.

## 2. Target — CONFIRMED (`metrics.target`)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Column | `is_fraud` |
| Positive meaning | "A payment the account holder would not have wanted to complete — account/device takeover, or a payment the user was socially engineered into initiating." |
| Negative meaning | "A payment the account holder genuinely intended, INCLUDING large, unusual or first-time payments that merely look suspicious." |
| Decision-time statement | "Predicted at payment submission, before the transaction settles. Any signal unavailable at that instant is leakage." |

## 8. Calibration — CONFIRMED

Method: **isotonic**, fitted on validation. `raw_ece = 0.01836`,
`calibrated_ece ≈ 1.13e-19` (this in-sample figure is explicitly flagged
in the artifact's own rationale as optimistically biased — isotonic
regression can fit its own training data almost exactly). The
metadata's own rationale states: *"Treat the held-out test ECE as the
only trustworthy calibration estimate"* — that is the test-set ECE of
**0.002530** reported in §7.4. Applied because the improvement exceeded
the 0.005 threshold.
